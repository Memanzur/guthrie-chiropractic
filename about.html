About Us

Over 30 years of helping West Jordan families live pain-free.

Meet Dr. Dan Guthrie

Dr. Dan Guthrie was raised in Mountain Home, Idaho and was introduced to chiropractic adjusting at just 13 years old. That early experience set him on a path that would become his life's work.

After serving an LDS mission from 1980 to 1982, Dr. Guthrie continued his education at Ricks College and Brigham Young University before graduating from Western States Chiropractic College in 1993.

Since then, Dr. Guthrie has been practicing chiropractic care right here in Utah, building over 30 years of clinical experience helping patients find lasting relief from pain.

His approach is simple: get you out of pain and help you understand how to keep the problem from coming back. He believes in treating the whole person, not just the symptoms.
