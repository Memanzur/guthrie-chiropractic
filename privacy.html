Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

Last updated: May 7, 2026

Who We Are

Guthrie Chiropractic Physicians ("we," "our," or "us") operates this website. Our office is located at 8822 South Redwood Rd, Suite C212, West Jordan, UT 84088. You can reach us at (801) 957-9696.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Fill out our contact form (name, email address, phone number, message)
  • Submit your email address through our wellness quiz
  • Call or email our office directly

We do not collect health insurance information, Social Security numbers, or medical records through this website.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and messages
  • Schedule appointments
  • Send you the wellness report you requested
  • Communicate with you about our services

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

This website uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts for typography. Google may collect usage data when fonts are loaded. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps to display our office location. Google may collect usage data through the embedded map. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Formspree to process contact form submissions. See Formspree's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Cookies

This website does not set its own cookies. However, the third-party services listed above (Google Fonts, Google Maps) may place cookies on your device. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you provide. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of your data.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we have about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete your personal information
  • Opt out of receiving communications from us

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at (801) 957-9696 or through our contact form.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us so we can delete it.

HIPAA Notice

This website is not used to transmit, store, or process protected health information (PHI). Any health-related information you share through our contact form or quiz is general in nature and is not part of your medical record. Your medical records are maintained separately at our office in accordance with HIPAA regulations. For a copy of our Notice of Privacy Practices regarding your medical records, please contact our office directly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Your continued use of this website after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
